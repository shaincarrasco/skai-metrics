Evaluation Metrics and Rewards for AI-Based Telescope Schedulers

The metric

teff = η2 · FWHMfidFWHM2 · bdarkb

η cloud transmission FWHM delivered blur vs the band's fiducial b sky brightness vs dark sky

Effective exposure time discounts an exposure's clock time by everything the atmosphere and the sky take away: blur spreads a star's light over more pixels, clouds absorb photons before they arrive, and a bright sky buries faint sources in noise. An exposure taken in poor conditions is worth only a fraction of its wall-clock seconds; one taken in exceptional conditions can be worth more than its face value. A schedule's goodness, then, is simply how much of the available night it converts into effective science seconds — which makes teff a natural reward signal for an AI scheduler.
